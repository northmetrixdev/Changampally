Our Ayurvedic Departments

This department adopts an integrated approach, combining the principles of Ayurveda with conventional medicine to manage cancers and other complex diseases. The focal focus is on evidence-based approaches to root out the causes of diseases.

Our Departments
Oncology

Specializes in the diagnosis, supportive treatment, and rehabilitation of various types of cancers.

Diseases: Brain tumor | Breast cancer | Cervical cancer | Ovarian cancer | Lung cancer | Bone cancer | Skin cancer | Gastrointestinal cancer | Blood cancer | Prostate cancer

Neurology

Specializes in diagnosing and treating disorders of the nervous system including the brain, spinal cord and nerves

Diseases: Stroke | Memory disorders | Dementia | Alzheimer's | Parkinson's | Multiple sclerosis (MS) | Epilepsy | Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) | Autism spectrum disorder (ASD) | Cerebral palsy

Rheumatology and Immunology

This department specializes in diagnosing and treating diseases that affect the joints, muscles, bones and disorders of the immune system.

Diseases: Rheumatoid arthritis (RA) | Systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) | Type 1 diabetes mellitus | Psoriasis | Hashimoto's thyroiditis

Endocrinology and Metabolism

This department specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of disorders related to hormones, glands, and the body's metabolism.

Diseases: Diabetes mellitus (Type 1 and Type 2) | Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) | Metabolic syndrome | Thyroid disorders (Hypothyroidism, Hyperthyroidism) | Cushing's syndrome | Adrenal insufficiency | Osteoporosis

Cardiology

Treats heart and vascular system disorders, focusing on maintaining cardiovascular health

Diseases: Hypertension (high blood pressure) | Coronary Artery Disease (CAD) | Arrhythmias (Atrial fibrillation | Ventricular tachycardia) | Peripheral artery disease (PAD)

Gastroenterology

This department focuses on the diagnosis and treatment of disorders affecting the digestive system, including the esophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine, liver, gallbladder, and pancreas.

Diseases: Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) | Crohn's disease | Gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD) | Alcoholic liver cirrhosis | Celiac disease | Chronic pancreatitis | Peptic ulcer disease

Psychiatry and Psychology

An integrated approach to diagnosing and treating mental, emotional, and behavioral disorders, focusing on both psychological well-being and psychiatric conditions.

Diseases: Major depressive disorder (MDD) | Generalized anxiety disorder (GAD) | Bipolar disorder | Obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD) | Post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) | Eating disorders (Anorexia nervosa, Bulimia) | Digital dependence syndrome

Pulmonology

Pulmonology is a medical specialty that deals with diseases involving the respiratory tract. It focuses on the diagnosis and treatment of lung conditions.

Diseases: Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) | Asthma | Other complicated lung diseases

Orthopedics and Musculoskeletal

This department specializes in the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of disorders affecting bones, joints, ligaments, tendons, muscles, and nerves.

Diseases: Osteoarthritis (OA) | Ankylosing spondylitis | Gout | Spondyloarthropathies (Shoulder, neck, upper and lower back aches) | Sciatica

Ophthalmology

The Ophthalmology department specializes in eyes and visual system. Our experts diagnose, treat, and prevent a wide range of eye conditions, from common refractive errors to complex diseases.

Diseases: Myopia (Nearsightedness) | Astigmatism | Glaucoma | Inflammatory Eye Diseases | Retinitis Pigmentosa

Hematology

The Hematology Department specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of blood disorders, including conditions like Thrombocytopenia, Chronic Anemia, and various other blood-related ailments.

Diseases: Thrombocytopenia | Chronic anemia | Other blood disorders

Dermatology

The Dermatology Department specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of skin, hair, and nail conditions. This includes common issues like acne and eczema, as well as more complex diseases such as psoriasis and skin cancer.

Diseases: Atopic dermatitis (Eczema) | Psoriasis | Chronic urticaria

Dental Oncology

A Dental Oncology department specializes in the oral health care of cancer patients. This involves the diagnosis and management of cancers affecting the teeth, gums, tongue, jaw, and surrounding tissues.

Diseases: Not specified

Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation

The Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation helps you regain strength, mobility, and quality of life. We provide personalized care for a wide range of musculoskeletal, neurological and pain related conditions.

Diseases: Interventional Pain Management | Neurorehabilitation | Critical Care Rehabilitation | Musculoskeletal Rehabilitation | Rheumatological Rehabilitation | Sports and Regenerative Medicine | Orthopedic Rehabilitation | Sexual Rehabilitation
